Standard municipal tariff in Indian urban regions typically ranges between ₹10 to ₹25 per kilo-litre (1,000 L).
Mathematical Calculation:
Charge = (Consumption in Litres / 1,000) × Tariff
e.g. (12480 / 1,000) × ₹15.0 = ₹187.20
