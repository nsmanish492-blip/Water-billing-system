Principle of Hydraulic Differential Analysis SERIES TOPOLOGY REQUIRED

Crucial Engineering Concept: Comparing two flow sensor readings can only indicate possible pipe leakage if the sensors are positioned in a series arrangement along the same fluid conduit (i.e., Inlet Meter ➔ Pipe Section Under Test ➔ Outlet Meter).

If sensors are installed on separate parallel branches or distinct plumbing fixtures, their numerical difference reflects divergent fixture consumption, never a leak. This system strictly labels findings as "Possible abnormal flow — inspect the pipe" rather than an unverified "confirmed leak".
